Your TV, quietly hung with masterpieces.

Minimal Art turns your Samsung Smart TV into a calm, ad-free gallery: full-screen paintings by Monet, Van Gogh, Renoir and Turner, a clean clock, today's weather and a slim column of app shortcuts.

What it does

60 masterpieces built in

Public-domain paintings from the National Gallery of Art's Open Access collection, stored inside the app.

Your gallery, your pace

Pick the artworks you like and change them every minute, every hour or anything in between.

Clock and weather

12 or 24-hour time and the local forecast in °F or °C, for 18 cities.

App shortcuts

Up to 8 favorite apps and live TV, one press away with your remote.

Gallery mode

Leave the remote alone and the menu fades, leaving only the painting.

No ads, no tracking

No account, no analytics, no data collection. Read the privacy policy.
